# Cinematic Developer Learning

Editorial clarity with digital depth: retain Inter and the existing violet primary, combine neutral surfaces with small cyan/blue lighting accents, and keep all body text on calm flat surfaces. No invented metrics, reading times, progress, disabled fake controls or new educational claims.

| Intensity | Placement | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Homepage and curriculum introduction | Layered developer workspace, decorative perspective planes, static radial light, clear real lesson links |
| Moderate | Group headers and navigable cards | Cohesive numbered group identity, compact surfaces, fine-pointer hover lift, equivalent focus emphasis |
| Low | Article hero, code, concept visuals and callouts | Compact editorial hero, surface edges and restrained shadows; flat selectable code and unchanged conceptual relationships |

No continuous animation behind reading content. No pointer-tracking React state. Essential content is visible at first paint and without motion. Decorative markup is hidden from assistive technology and cannot intercept pointer events. Motion is limited to CSS transforms/opacity and bounded decorative entrance/scroll effects; reduced motion disables it. Light theme uses quieter lighting and neutral shadows rather than copying dark glow.

Prototype acceptance: real CTAs reachable immediately on mobile; no transformed overflow; article controls and initial lesson navigation remain near the top; no new client JS imports; text/controls maintain contrast. Validate on 375 and 1440 in both themes before shared rollout. Subjective polish is not evidence of increased engagement.
